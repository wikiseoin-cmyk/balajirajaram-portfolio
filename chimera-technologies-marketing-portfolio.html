The Journey

From SEO execution to integrated B2B marketing

My role at Chimera Technologies evolved significantly over six years. I started with SEO and website optimization and gradually expanded into content, social media, website strategy, inbound marketing, reputation management, sales enablement, and broader marketing execution.

2020–2021

Building the SEO & Website Foundation

My first year focused on improving the website structure, organic search visibility, website experience, social media, and local search presence.

Key Contributions

  • Recommended and coordinated website improvements including CTA elements, Back to Top functionality, URL restructuring, custom pages, case study layouts, and featured services sections.
  • Expanded organic search visibility from 5 ranking keywords to 108 keywords.
  • Achieved 50 first-page Google keyword rankings.
  • Increased website backlinks from approximately 687 to 15,676.
  • Added 18 new landing pages while coordinating development and content requirements.
  • Managed regular social media publishing and group sharing.
  • Managed Google Business Profile activities including existing location claims and new US and Singapore locations.
  • Supported careers-page updates and social promotion of job openings.
  • Worked with the team to accelerate the website revamp process.

Manager Recognition

Recognized for exceeding expectations, particularly in SEO and independent execution. The website moved from very limited keyword visibility to achieving 15+ first-page keyword rankings.


I was also appreciated for proactively managing social media without dedicated support from a content writer or graphic designer.

2021–2022

Expanding Organic Visibility & Digital Marketing

The focus expanded from SEO execution into international search visibility, website content, social media planning, and online reputation management.

Key Contributions

  • Added approximately 30 new ranking keywords, taking total ranking keywords to around 130.
  • Added 11 landing pages, 6 case studies, and 4 news pages.
  • Increased backlinks from approximately 15,676 to 41,200.
  • Created 20 US location-focused pages as a pilot international SEO initiative.
  • Prepared and executed the digital marketing calendar.
  • Designed and published social media content independently.
  • Supported online reputation management and review-generation activities.

Manager Recognition

Recognized as an important contributor who could independently manage multiple areas of marketing.


Particular appreciation was given to lead generation, website optimization, social media content creation, and reputation management.
